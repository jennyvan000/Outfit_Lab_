Who are you today?

Most outfit apps make you look nice. This one makes you look somewhere new: two aesthetics that don't belong together, one outfit built from your own closet.

00Opposite attractsTHE CLASH MACHINE

Pick any two aesthetics that have nothing in common. Your job: make them live in one outfit. It's a creative exercise to push your fashion identity somewhere you wouldn't go on your own.

1Spin

We randomly pull two aesthetics that clash, e.g. Coquette × Grunge.

2Combine

Build one outfit with at least 1 piece from each, finished with your own wardrobe. Use the bridge ideas if you're stuck.

3Save

Send it to your lookbook or post it, and write down what surprised you.

    01Your closetCLOSET

    Drop photos into the matching box (or tap a box to choose files). No auto-sorting needed. Flat-lay on a plain background works best.

    Model photo (optional)

    Upload a photo of yourself or a friend. It appears in the centre of the spread, cut into a paper-cut shape, like a magazine cover. Your clothes stack around it as tagged items.

    Tip: add several items in the same category and the generator will shuffle between your real pieces.
